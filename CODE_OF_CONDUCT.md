## 🇬🇧 English

### Our pledge

We pledge to make participating in Zoo Framework welcoming, safe, and equitable for all,
regardless of characteristics including race, ethnicity, caste, colour, age, physical
characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression,
sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin,
socio-economic position, level of education, or other status. The same privileges of
participation are extended to everyone who participates in good faith and in accordance
with this Covenant.

### Encouraged behaviours

1. Respecting the **purpose of our community** — a framework that deliberately declines
   features. "No" is a design decision here, not a judgement about you.
2. Engaging **kindly and honestly** with others.
3. Respecting **different viewpoints** and experiences — a newcomer's question is the same
   mechanism as the framework's own "fail loudly on bad input", not an imposition.
4. **Taking responsibility** for our actions and contributions.
5. Gracefully giving and accepting **constructive feedback** — about the work, not the person.
6. Committing to **repairing harm** when it occurs.
7. Behaving in other ways that promote and sustain the **well-being of our community**.

### Restricted behaviours

Instances, threats and promotion of the following are violations of this Code of Conduct.

1. **Harassment** — violating explicitly expressed boundaries, or engaging in unnecessary
   personal attention after any clear request to stop.
2. **Character attacks** — insulting, demeaning or pejorative comments at a community
   member or group.
3. **Stereotyping or discrimination** — characterising anyone by immutable identities or traits.
4. **Sexualisation** — behaviour that would generally be considered inappropriately intimate
   in the context or purpose of this community.
5. **Violating confidentiality** — sharing or acting on someone's private information
   without their permission.
6. **Endangerment** — causing, encouraging or threatening violence or other harm.
7. Behaving in other ways that **threaten the well-being** of our community.

**Other restrictions**

1. **Misleading identity** — impersonating someone, or pretending to be someone else to
   evade enforcement.
2. **Failing to credit sources** of content you contribute.
3. **Promotional materials** shared outside the norms of this community.
4. **Irresponsible communication** — presenting content that includes, links or describes
   any restricted behaviour.

### Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, review-thread discussion) and
whenever someone is officially representing the project in public.

### Reporting an issue

Report a possible violation to **mengxiang931015@live.com**, starting the subject line with
`[Code of Conduct]`. Reports are read by the moderator only, and the reporter's identity is
not disclosed without their consent.

**This is a different route from security reporting.** Vulnerabilities must not come through
here: they go to the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — a GitHub private
security advisory first and the same email second. Mixing the two routes loses a report.

Platform-level harassment (spam, impersonation, coordinated abuse) can additionally be
reported to GitHub directly through the **Report abuse** link on the relevant profile,
comment or repository — that route does not depend on this project's maintainer at all.

### Addressing and repairing harm

Where a violation is found, the following four-rung ladder is used to decide how best to
repair the harm, based on the incident's impact on the people involved and the community.
Lower rungs may be skipped for severe violations.

1. **Warning**
   - *Event*: a single incident, or a short series of incidents.
   - *Consequence*: a private, written warning.
   - *Repair*: for example a private written apology, acknowledgement of responsibility,
     and clarifying expectations.
2. **Temporarily limited activities**
   - *Event*: a repeated violation that already drew a warning, or the first instance of a
     more serious violation.
   - *Consequence*: a private written warning plus a time-limited cooldown, which may be
     limited to particular communication channels or particular people.
   - *Repair*: making an apology, using the cooldown to reflect on actions and impact, and
     re-entering community spaces thoughtfully afterwards.
3. **Temporary suspension**
   - *Event*: a pattern of violations that warnings have not resolved, or a single serious
     violation.
   - *Consequence*: a private written warning stating the conditions for return.
   - *Repair*: respecting the spirit of the suspension, meeting the stated conditions, and
     reintegrating thoughtfully when it is lifted.
4. **Permanent ban**
   - *Event*: a pattern that the other rungs failed to resolve, or a violation so serious
     that the community cannot be kept safe with this person as a member.
   - *Consequence*: access to all project spaces, tools and communication channels is removed.
   - *Repair*: there is no possible repair at this severity.

The ladder is a guideline, not a straitjacket: it does not remove the moderator's judgement.

### Who moderates — and one honest limitation

Contributor Covenant 3.0 speaks of *Community Moderators*. Zoo Framework currently has
**one maintainer**, who acts as the moderator. If the maintainer is the subject of a report,
they recuse themselves — and because there is **no second maintainer and no independent
third party**, nobody else can receive that report. It is nevertheless acknowledged, and
the outcome is stated publicly without disclosing the reporter.

## 🇨🇳 中文

### 我们的承诺

我们希望参与 Zoo Framework 对每个人都是友好、安全且公平的体验，不因种族、族裔、种姓、
肤色、年龄、身体特征、神经多样性、残障、生理性别或社会性别、性别认同与表达、性取向、
语言、哲学或宗教、民族或社会出身、社会经济地位、教育程度或其他身份而有所区别。
对每一个善意参与、并遵守本准则的人，参与的特权完全相同。

### 鼓励的行为

1. 尊重**本社区的目的** —— 本框架会主动拒绝功能；这里的「不」是设计决策，不是对你个人的
   评价。
2. **友善且诚实**地与他人相处。
3. 尊重**不同的观点**与经历 —— 新手的提问，与框架自身「错误输入就大声失败」是同一套机制，
   不是一种打扰。
4. 对自己的行为与贡献**负责**。
5. 得体地给出和接受**建设性反馈** —— 只针对工作本身，不针对人。
6. 在造成伤害时，承诺**修复损害**。
7. 以其他有助于**社区福祉**的方式行事。

### 受限的行为

下列行为的实施、威胁与宣扬，均属违反本准则：

1. **骚扰** —— 违背对方明确表达的边界，或在对方明确要求停止后仍进行不必要的个人关注。
2. **人身攻击** —— 针对社区成员或群体发表侮辱、贬低或轻蔑的言论。
3. **刻板印象或歧视** —— 依据不可改变的身份或特征去评判他人的性格或行为。
4. **性化** —— 在本社区的语境或目的下，通常会被视为不适当亲密的言行。
5. **侵犯隐私** —— 未经许可分享或利用他人的私人信息。
6. **危害他人** —— 造成、鼓励或威胁暴力及其他伤害。
7. 以其他方式**威胁社区福祉**。

**其他限制**

1. **误导性身份** —— 冒充他人，或为逃避处理而假扮他人。
2. **未注明来源** —— 未恰当标注你所贡献内容的来源。
3. **推广内容** —— 以超出本社区常规的方式发布营销或其他商业内容。
4. **不负责任的传播** —— 呈现、链接或描述任何受限行为的内容。

### 适用范围

适用于所有项目空间（issue、PR、评审线程中的讨论），以及任何人在公开场合正式代表本项目时。

### 报告问题

请将可能的违规行为报告到 **mengxiang931015@live.com**，并在**邮件主题开头**加
`[Code of Conduct]`。报告仅由处理者本人阅读，未经报告者同意不披露其身份。

**这条路由与安全漏洞报送不同。** 漏洞**不要**走这里：它走 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
描述的私密渠道——优先 GitHub 私密安全通告，其次才是同一个邮箱。两条路由混在一起会丢报告。

平台层面的骚扰（垃圾信息、冒充身份、有组织的攻击）还可以直接向 GitHub 举报：在相关
账号、评论或仓库上使用 **Report abuse**（举报滥用）入口，那条路完全不依赖本项目的维护者。

### 处理与损害修复

确认违规后，按下述**四级阶梯**决定如何最好地修复损害，依据是该事件对当事人与社区的影响。
情节严重时可以跳过较低的层级。

1. **警告**
   - *事件*：单次事件，或短时间内的连续几次事件。
   - *后果*：一份私下的书面警告。
   - *修复*：例如私下书面道歉、承认责任，并就期望达成一致。
2. **临时限制活动**
   - *事件*：已被警告过的重复违规，或首次出现的较严重违规。
   - *后果*：一份私下书面警告，附带限时的冷却期；冷却期可以限定于特定沟通渠道或特定人员。
   - *修复*：道歉、利用冷却期反思行为与影响，冷却结束后有分寸地重新参与社区。
3. **临时封禁**
   - *事件*：经多次警告仍未解决的模式性违规，或单次严重违规。
   - *后果*：一份私下书面警告，写明恢复参与的条件。
   - *修复*：尊重封禁的用意、满足所设条件，并在解封后谨慎地重新融入。
4. **永久封禁**
   - *事件*：其他层级均未能解决的模式性违规，或严重到无法在保留此人作为成员的前提下维持
     社区安全的违规。
   - *后果*：移除其对全部项目空间、工具与沟通渠道的访问权。
   - *修复*：此严重程度下不存在可能的修复。

阶梯是指导而非枷锁：它不取消处理者的判断空间。

### 由谁处理 —— 以及一处如实说明

Contributor Covenant 3.0 的术语是 *Community Moderators*（社区处理者）。Zoo Framework
目前**只有一位维护者**，由维护者本人充当处理者。如果报告的对象是维护者本人，维护者应当
回避；而因为**没有第二位维护者、也不存在独立的第三方**，此时没有任何其他人能够受理该报告。
这种情况下仍会确认收到报告，并在不披露报告者身份的前提下公开说明处理结果。

## 按规模精简之处

本准则**不是** Contributor Covenant 3.0 的逐字全文，如实列出与官方文本的差异：

- **执行主体**：官方文本称 *Community Moderators*；本项目只有一位维护者，故由维护者本人
  担任，且明确写明「维护者本人是报告对象时不存在独立第三方」这一限制。
- **执行阶梯**：保留官方的四级阶梯与每级的 Event / Consequence / Repair 三要素，未做增删；
  但四级全部由同一位处理者裁量，没有多人合议环节。
- **举报方式**：官方模板要求填写举报方式，此处填入邮箱与主题前缀（见上）。
- **本地条目**：官方 3.0 的鼓励行为与受限行为以通用表述为主；本准则在「鼓励的行为」第 1、3
  条中加入了本项目的具体语境（主动拒绝功能、提问不是打扰），其余条目与官方保持同义。
